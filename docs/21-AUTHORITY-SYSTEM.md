## 9. Anchor-text discipline

When you have control of inbound link text:
- **Branded** (40%): "Pura Nights", "Melitta Siomos", "Pura Nights London".
- **Naked URL** (15%): `puranights.com`.
- **Long-tail descriptive** (35%): "salsa classes in Chiswick", "bachata classes in Ealing", "wedding dance lessons in West London".
- **Generic** (10%): "click here", "learn more", "this guide".

Avoid >2 exact-match keyword anchors per quarter. Over-optimisation triggers manual review risk.
