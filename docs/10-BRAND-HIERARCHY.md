# Brand Hierarchy & Identity — Melitta Siomos / Pura Nights

---

## Brand Architecture

```
Melitta Siomos (Master Personal Brand)
├── Pura Nights (Weekly Classes & Community)
├── Pura Ladies (Performance Team)
└── Wedding Dance Made Easy (Wedding Specialist)
```

### How Each Brand Appears

| Brand | Where It Leads | Tone | Visual Identity |
|-------|---------------|------|----------------|
| **Melitta Siomos** | Authority, credibility, founder story | Professional, warm, expert | Personal photos, bio, credentials |
| **Pura Nights** | Weekly classes, social dancing, community | Vibrant, welcoming, energetic | Gold/dark palette, dance imagery |
| **Pura Ladies** | Performance team, aspirational dance | Aspirational, elegant, powerful | Team photos, performance shots |
| **Wedding Dance Made Easy** | Wedding first dance lessons | Premium, reassuring, romantic | Couple imagery, soft tones |

### Brand Hierarchy Rules

1. **Homepage**: Pura Nights is the primary brand. Melitta appears in "Meet Your Instructor" section.
2. **About page**: Melitta Siomos is the H1 focus. Pura Nights, Pura Ladies, and Wedding Dance are sub-sections.
3. **Service pages**: Each sub-brand owns its page (e.g. `/pura-ladies` is fully Pura Ladies branded).
4. **Blog**: Author is always "Melitta Siomos". Blog brand is Pura Nights.
5. **Footer**: All four brands appear with their respective Instagram links.
6. **Navigation**: Pura Nights is the site name. Melitta appears on About and author cards only.
