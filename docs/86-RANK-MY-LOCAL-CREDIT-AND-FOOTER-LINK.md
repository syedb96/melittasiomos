- Dedicated page: `/website-credits` (canonical, OG, WebPage schema, indexable).
- Existing `/credits` page also references Rank My Local but is broader (covers
  content, photography, third-party services). Both remain in the footer.
- Footer bottom bar carries a subtle line:
  `Website design & local SEO support by Rank My Local`
  pointing at `https://rankmylocal.co.uk/` with `target="_blank"` and
  `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Accessible label: "Visit Rank My Local website".

## What it does NOT do

- Not in the top navigation.
- No sitewide promotional banners for Rank My Local.
- No claims that Rank My Local owns or runs Pura Nights — scoped strictly to
  "website design, SEO structure and launch support".
